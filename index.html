Website Instructions

  • Search Bar — find clubs, students, or posts across schools.
  • Filter system — narrow the feed or a club page by school, topic, date, or post type.
  • Reels — short-form video posts, swipe through the horizontal row.
  • Report system — flag a post from its ⋮ menu; staff review all reports.
